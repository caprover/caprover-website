slug: /applications/tags-descriptions
---

Add an app description to explain its purpose and operational owner. Add tags when you want to group or filter services in the dashboard. Both are stored with the app definition; they do not change its Docker networking, resource isolation, or deployment behavior.

Projects give apps a nested organizational location. Tags can cut across that hierarchy, such as `customer-facing`, `staging`, or a team name. Keep names consistent so other operators can find an app quickly. See [Organize Apps with Projects](./projects.md).
